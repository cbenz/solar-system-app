# 05: POV

**What to build:** One observer stands on the Earth as a coloured dot, and a panel at the edge of the screen shows what that person sees: a horizon, a sky, and the Sun. The panel darkens the moment the dot crosses into the shadow.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Time

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The panel's border carries the same colour as its dot
- [ ] Daylight is derived from the Sun's elevation above the horizon, never from a clock
- [ ] The dot crossing the terminator and the panel going dark happen at the same instant
- [ ] The Sun is visible in the panel and sets behind the horizon
- [ ] A sky is visible above the horizon after dark
