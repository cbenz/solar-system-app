# 06: Placing observers

**What to build:** The child taps anywhere on the globe to put an observer there, or drags an existing one, and the place names itself. The camera holds still for the whole gesture.

**Blocked by:** 05 — POV

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Tapping anywhere on the visible globe places the observer at that latitude
- [ ] Dragging moves the observer along its meridian only
- [ ] The camera does not move during a drag or a tap
- [ ] The place is labelled with its geographic name, computed from the latitude rather than chosen from a list
- [ ] The Equator is drawn on the Earth at all times
- [ ] A tap on the far side of the globe does not place an observer
