# 12: Shadows

**What to build:** A stick in the ground and its shadow, readable as a clock, then watched across a year until it stops being a clock and starts being evidence.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Placing observers

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A stick and its shadow are visible in the POV
- [ ] The shadow is longest at sunrise and sunset, shortest at noon, and points the other way in the afternoon
- [ ] A mission asks the child to find the shortest shadow and answers in the scene
- [ ] Over a year, the noon shadow changes length at the same place
- [ ] The shadow's direction at noon changes across the year
