# 15: Sandbox editing

**What to build:** The sandbox stops being an observatory and becomes a workbench: bodies can be dragged off their orbits and their properties edited, and the orbit recomputes as a consequence.

**Blocked by:** 07 — Activities as data

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A properties card per body opens from the toolbar, and editing requires pressing the pencil first
- [ ] Mass, distance and speed are editable, and the orbit changes accordingly
- [ ] A body can be dragged off its orbit and lands on a new one
- [ ] A body dropped with no velocity follows a documented convention rather than an undefined one
- [ ] Launching the Moon fast enough makes it leave and not come back
- [ ] Scrubbing stays reversible after any edit
