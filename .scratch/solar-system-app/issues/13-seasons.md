# 13: Seasons

**What to build:** The child's own belief that summer comes from being closer to the Sun is put on the table, then destroyed by evidence the child produced, and the tilt is what remains.

**Blocked by:** 11 — Latitudes, 12 — Shadows

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Paris and Sydney are visible at the same instant, one in summer and one in winter, both the same distance from the Sun
- [ ] The child can state the distance explanation, and the app then tests it instead of contradicting it
- [ ] The app shows the Earth nearest the Sun in early January
- [ ] A tilt slider reaches zero, and the seasons shrink to a small remainder rather than disappearing
- [ ] Sunlight is drawn as parallel rays striking the Earth
- [ ] An exaggerated orbit is available, and demonstrably cannot produce opposite seasons
- [ ] No visibly elliptical orbit is drawn while the seasons are being explained
