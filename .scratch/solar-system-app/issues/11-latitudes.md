# 11: Latitudes

**What to build:** Three places on the same planet, seen at once over a whole year: the Equator, a Tropic and the Polar Circle. This is the activity that makes the tiled POV strip real.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Placing observers, 07 — Activities as data

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The activity declares three POVs and they are tiled flush, without gaps
- [ ] In landscape on a phone the POVs move to a side column
- [ ] The time span is one year
- [ ] At the Equator every day lasts about twelve hours, all year
- [ ] At the Polar Circle the polar night begins exactly there and nowhere below
- [ ] At the Tropic the Sun reaches the zenith on exactly one day of the year
- [ ] The Tropics and the Polar Circles are drawn on the Earth in this activity
