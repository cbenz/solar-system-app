# 09: Missions

**What to build:** A mission asks the child to predict, then act on the model, then look at what happened. The model answers in the scene. Nothing is ever a form or a multiple-choice question.

**Blocked by:** 07 — Activities as data

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The prediction is recorded before the reveal, and a wrong prediction is accepted and kept
- [ ] The answer arrives as something visible in the scene, not as a score or a verdict
- [ ] Where the task is continuous, the feedback distinguishes close from right
- [ ] No multiple-choice or free-text control exists anywhere in the app
- [ ] A mission can be abandoned at any point with nothing lost
